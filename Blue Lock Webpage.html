About Blue Lock

Blue Lock is a Japanese football anime and manga about creating the ultimate egoist striker for the Japanese national football team. The Blue Lock program isolates 300 young players and forces them to compete in intense psychological and physical tests.

The Story

After Japan is eliminated from the World Cup, the Japan Football Union hires Jinpachi Ego to create the world’s best striker. The Blue Lock facility becomes a battlefield of ambition where only one player can emerge as the definitive star.

The Books:

Book 1:

The Blue Lock project begins as 300 strikers are gathered to forge Japan’s ultimate egoist forward. Yoichi Isagi enters the ruthless program and starts competing within Team Z.

Book 2:

Team Z struggles to unite as they face stronger opponents in the First Selection. Isagi begins honing his spatial awareness to give his team a fighting chance.

Book 3:

Team Z battles Team Y and then Team W, where betrayal and desperation push them to their limits. Isagi starts to awaken his ego and take decisive actions on the field.

Book 4:

Team Z confronts the powerhouse Team V in a do-or-die match. Isagi and his teammates unlock new abilities as they fight to overcome V’s overwhelming talent.

Book 5:

The match against Team V reaches a dramatic climax as Isagi and Nagi clash in a battle of evolving instincts. Team Z pushes beyond its limits in hopes of surviving Blue Lock’s First Selection.
